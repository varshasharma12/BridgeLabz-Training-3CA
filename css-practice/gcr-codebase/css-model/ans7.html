The Power of Good Typography

Typography plays an important role in making a webpage easy to read and visually attractive. A good combination of font family, font size and spacing helps readers understand content quickly. Strong text creates emphasis, while italic text adds a different style and feeling to the content. Proper line-height also gives enough space between lines and improves readability.

Arial is a clean and simple font that works well for normal website content and headings.

Courier New is a monospace font often used for code, technical information and examples.
